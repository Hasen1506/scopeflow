# WS-03: invoicing, GST documents, TDS, MSMED and evidence packs

What changed, why, and what an operator has to do. Everything that computes tax, TDS or interest is
an **estimate based on the rules as we understand them on 12 Oct 2026: confirm with your CA.** The
open points for a CA are listed at the end.

## Behaviour changes

### Invoices can never exceed the agreement (C2)
- `invoiceable` = agreement total with tax (signed baseline + approved change orders, integer minor
  units, the project's own tax-head logic) minus every **live** invoice across all baselines (an
  invoice fully cancelled by a credit note no longer counts) and minus advances already taxed on a
  receipt voucher.
- A new invoice beyond it is refused with **409 `exceeds_uninvoiced`** and the copy
  "Only ₹X is left to invoice on this agreement." (`invoiceable` in the body, major units).
  Previously the cap was the *unpaid balance*, so a fully invoiced but unpaid agreement could be
  invoiced again without limit; a paid-in-full agreement used to give 400 and now gives 409.
- The check and the invoice-number allocation run in one transaction holding
  `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the project row (`withProjectLock`), so parallel requests cannot both pass.
- The last invoice may come out a paisa per head above the remainder from per-head rounding; it is
  capped with an explicit negative round-off line.
- `invoiceable(project, ledger)` (lib/invoice-server.ts) and the pure `computeInvoiceable(agreement, docs)`
  (lib/invoice-cap.ts) are exported for WS-04 checkout and WS-10 MCP.

### Amount entry and GST per head (M1 at invoice level, plan §9.3)
- **The invoice amount is now the taxable value by default** (GST added on top). Send
  `includes_tax: true` for an amount that already includes GST. API clients that sent tax-inclusive
  amounts must add `includes_tax: true` (the owner panel has the toggle; milestone and change-order
  defaults are tax-inclusive and set it themselves).
- Heads are computed from the taxable value: CGST = SGST = round(taxable × rate/2), IGST =
  round(taxable × rate); inclusive entry puts any residual paisa on an explicit **Round off** line.
- New invoice snapshots carry `entry`, `gross_taxable`, `breakdown` (minor units), and when relevant
  `advance`, `tds`, `change_order`, `cap`. Snapshots issued before WS-03 render exactly as before.
- The engine is `lib/invoice-gst.ts` until WS-01's `lib/gst.ts` `gstFromExclusive/gstFromInclusive`
  (PR #26) land on main; it accepts WS-01's `SupplyType` names (`'none'` = one undivided head) and
  `labeledHeads()` returns WS-01's `{name, label, rate, amount}` head shape, so the swap is an import
  change. `tests/golden/ws-03-gst.golden.json` pins the figures for that swap.

### Receipt vouchers for advances (H11)
- An advance (an online deposit, a payment recorded with "deposit"/"advance" in its note or
  reference, or money received before the project's first invoice) gets a **Receipt Voucher**
  (GST rule 50) in its own series **RV-0001**, with tax on the advance at the agreement's rate. A
  seller without a GSTIN gets a plain **payment receipt PR-0001** instead.
- Vouchers are issued lazily, under the project lock, when the owner lists or issues invoices.
  Projects that already have pre-WS-03 invoices keep the old behaviour (no vouchers).
- The invoice that completes the agreement adjusts the advances: "Less: advance received vide
  RV-0001", tax only on the remainder. An ordinary invoice may cover only what is left beyond them.
- PDF: `GET /api/scopes/{id}/invoices/receipt-vouchers/{seq}`.

### Credit notes and the 16-character number (H12)
- `POST /api/scopes/{id}/credit-notes {invoice_seq, amount?, reason, request_id?}`: own series
  **CN-0001**, immutable (DB trigger), full or partial. A full credit marks the invoice
  "Cancelled by CN-0001" and frees its amount under the cap. PDF at `/credit-notes/{seq}`.
- Invoice, voucher and credit-note numbers must be at most 16 characters (GST rule 46); issuing is
  refused with 400 `invoice_number_too_long`. `invoicePrefixProblem()` gives the profile form the
  same rule (change request to WS-08 below).

### TDS as an explicit input (H19 / SF-08)
- `tds: {section_2025, rate_pct}` on an invoice, or as the project default via
  `PUT /api/scopes/{id}/invoices/terms`. **Never defaulted**: no section or rate is ever filled in.
- Sections follow the Income-tax Act 2025 s.393(1) Table Sl. 6; the old 194J/194C/194M labels are
  printed alongside for FY 2025-26 and earlier.
- TDS = rate × taxable value (GST excluded), rounded half up to the rupee. The invoice prints
  "TDS expected ₹X; net receivable ₹Y" with the CA banner.
- Statuses: `paid_net_of_tds` when the cash part is paid; `tds_credited` once the certificate is
  recorded (`POST /api/scopes/{id}/invoices/{seq}/tds-credit`). Reminders never chase the TDS part
  (`invoiceReminderDue` skips these statuses). Certificates pending are listed in the invoicing view
  (`tds.pending`, quarter and certificate due date).

### Change orders, milestones, allocation (M7, M9, M10)
- Every change order is shown as "+₹10,000.00 + GST ₹1,800.00 = ₹11,800.00" (`coDisplay`), and can be
  invoiced as its own line (`change_order_seq`) at its tax-inclusive total.
- Milestone amounts are frozen at baseline approval in `milestone_schedules` (minor units); approved
  change orders never move them. A milestone invoiced in full stays closed.
- One allocation function, `allocate()` (lib/invoice-status.ts): advances cover their receipt
  vouchers first; the rest FIFO by issue date, cash parts before TDS parts; refunds reduce the latest
  allocation; never negative. The statement, invoice statuses, the client page and the panel read it.

### PDFs (QA-01)
- Fonts and HarfBuzz load when the first invoicing route module loads; rendered (and signed) PDFs are
  cached for 10 minutes by the record's hash and streamed in 64 KB chunks.

### MSMED (SF-07)
- Udyam registration (number, date, category) via `PUT …/invoices/terms {msme_profile}` (stored in
  `msme_profiles` until WS-08's profile fields land; WS-08's columns win when present).
- `POST /api/scopes/{id}/acceptance` records delivery and written acceptance or an objection and its
  removal (s.2(b)); deemed acceptance after 15 days with no objection. Pay-by = acceptance + agreed
  days (at most 45) or 15 days with none agreed; interest runs from the next day.
- s.16 interest: compound with monthly rests at 3 × the RBI Bank Rate, daily within a month
  (rate ÷ 365), rounded to the paisa at each rest. The rate is read from the dated table
  `config/rbi-bank-rate.ts`; the basis is an input (`on_due_date`, the default, or `per_period`).
- Warnings: medium enterprises are excluded; registration after the agreement date: "MSMED benefits
  may not apply to work agreed before your Udyam registration (Silpi/Mahakali; NBCC reference pending)".
- `GET /api/scopes/{id}/dispute-pack?variant=msmed` adds the MSME Samadhaan field sheet, the interest
  worksheet (CSV + JSON with the rate table), and acceptance evidence. Public calculator at
  `/msme-interest`.
- CA worksheet: `tests/golden/ws-03-msmed-interest.worksheet.csv` (each period's opening balance,
  rate segments, interest and closing balance) for ₹1,00,000 due 1 Aug 2026 and paid 1 Dec 2026;
  the golden test recomputes it with independent plain arithmetic.

### Consumer evidence pack (SF-24)
- `?variant=consumer` (default in Consumer mode): plain-language summary, scope/BOQ, vendor
  acceptance receipts, change orders, stage payments with UTRs, inspections, timeline, MANIFEST.txt.

## Migration

`db/migrations/0020_ws-03_invoicing_compliance.sql` (additive, idempotent): tables `credit_notes`,
`receipt_vouchers`, `tds_credits`, `delivery_acceptances`, `milestone_schedules`, `invoice_terms`,
`msme_profiles` (immutability triggers on the issued documents), columns `invoices.tds_minor` and
`invoices.gross_minor`.

**Note:** it also replaces `invoices_amount_check` to allow `amount >= 0` (was `> 0`), because an
invoice whose whole value was paid in advance (adjusted from receipt vouchers) asks for ₹0. This is
the one non-additive constraint change; it only widens what is accepted.

## New env vars

None.

## Operator steps

1. Run migrations (`npm run db:migrate`; boot does it on Render).
2. When the RBI changes the Bank Rate (it moves with the MSF rate at each MPC decision), add a row to
   `config/rbi-bank-rate.ts` with the press-release URL.
3. Tell API users that invoice amounts are now the taxable value unless `includes_tax: true`.

## Open points for a CA

TDS base when GST is separate (we use the taxable value) and TDS rounding (nearest rupee); the
MSMED rate basis over a long delay and the day count (actual/365); GST per-head rounding and the
round-off line; receipt vouchers for advances on services; whether "_" may stay in invoice numbers
(rule 46 names "-" and "/" only; accepted for profiles saved before WS-03).
