# WS-04: Payments without custody (UPI direct, BYO accounts, Route-ready, escrow adapter)

Branch `hark/ws-04-payments`, based on `hark/ws-00-contracts` (Phase 0). Migration slot `0019`.
Primary: C3, C4, H7, H8, H9, H14, M20, M21, L4, L5, LT-02, LT-21, SF-02, SF-20. Supporting: H6 (alert
half, primary WS-05), M4 (primary WS-01). Background: IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md §11 (RBI Payment
Aggregator rules: a sole proprietorship must not collect clients' money and pass it on).

## Behaviour changes

### Client money always goes to the freelancer (C3, SF-20)
- `lib/payment-accounts.ts` `chooseRoute(accounts, currency, env)` (pure) picks where a client payment
  goes, in this order: INR → own Razorpay (Technology-Partner OAuth, then own API keys) → own Stripe →
  Route (flag); other currencies → own Stripe → own Razorpay. UPI (INR only) is offered next to it.
- **The operator's keys (`RAZORPAY_KEY_ID/SECRET`, `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`) never take a client payment in
  production** unless `SCOPEFLOW_ALLOW_OPERATOR_COLLECTION=1` (test/dev only). With only operator keys,
  `POST /api/review/<t>/deposit` (and invoice pay) answers **409 `payment_account_required`** before any
  provider call. Operator keys stay for ScopeFlow's own subscription billing (WS-08) and for lookups and
  refunds of payments they took before this change.
- Status of a route: `ready | upi_only | misconfigured | operator_refused | none`. The deposit view on the
  review page now also returns `open_minor`, `received_minor`, `status` and `upi`.
- Accounts (`scopeflow.payment_accounts`): `upi` (VPA + payee name), `razorpay_keys` (interim BYO keys),
  `razorpay_oauth` (Partner OAuth: `GET /api/payment-accounts/razorpay/connect` → Razorpay →
  `/api/payment-accounts/razorpay/callback`; orders/refunds use the sub-merchant access token, refreshed
  before expiry), `stripe_keys` (BYO secret or restricted key, optional currency list), `razorpay_route`
  (only with `SCOPEFLOW_ROUTE_ENABLED=1`: orders on ScopeFlow's keys with an `on_hold` transfer to the
  linked account). One active account per kind; disconnecting keeps the sealed secrets so the account can
  still refund what it took.
- Secrets are sealed with AES-256-GCM under `SCOPEFLOW_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (32 bytes, base64). API keys and
  OAuth tokens live in `secret_enc`; the **webhook signing secret in its own column `webhook_secret_box`**
  (NULL = none; WS-10's readiness probe counts those). On a localhost `APP_URL` a key is derived from
  `SCOPEFLOW_SIGNING_SECRET` so CI and dev work; anywhere else a missing key refuses to store secrets
  (503 `encryption_unavailable`).
- Per-account webhooks: `/api/payments/razorpay/webhook/<account id>` (BYO keys, verified with that
  account's secret) and `/api/payments/stripe/webhook/<account id>`; the platform Razorpay webhook accepts
  the operator secret (legacy + Route orders) or `RAZORPAY_PARTNER_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (OAuth accounts, matched
  by `account_id`). An account's webhook can only mark that account's orders paid.
- Read-only settlements/payouts per account: `GET /api/payment-accounts/<id>/settlements`.
- Refunds always go through the account that took the payment.
- Escrow: `lib/escrow/` (`create_escrow`, fund link, release on milestone approval, refund) with a stub
  (`SCOPEFLOW_ESCROW_PROVIDER=stub`, refused in production) and Castler / RazorpayX Escrow+ skeletons that
  refuse with `escrow_unavailable` until a company and provider agreement exist. No money path before that.

### UPI direct and "I've paid" (LT-02, SF-02)
- `lib/upi.ts`: `upiUri()` builds `upi://pay?pa=&pn=&am=&cu=INR&tn=&tr=` for the exact open amount (2 dp,
  `tn` ≤ 80 chars, `tr` ≤ 35); non-INR never gets a UPI URI.
- `GET /api/review/<t>/payments`: open targets (deposit, each open invoice, balance) in minor units, UPI
  URIs, bank details from the business profile, online availability and the client's claims.
- `POST /api/review/<t>/payment-claim`: the client reports a UPI/bank payment with its UTR (unique per
  project, masked back to the client). Nothing counts until the owner confirms on
  `POST /api/payments/claims/<id>` (`confirm` appends a `payment_received` entry once, ref `claim:<id>`;
  `reject` touches nothing). The owner is notified (`notify('payment_claim')`).
- Slots: `app/components/slots/pay-block.tsx` (QR via the `qrcode` dependency, UPI app link, bank details,
  own checkout when available, claim form) and `payment-settings.tsx` (accounts, claim inbox, alerts with a
  one-click refund for excess). WS-04's own pages: `/pay/<token>` and `/payments`.

### Money is project-scoped (C4, LT-21, M4)
- `lib/money-ledger.ts` `moneyState(entries, agreement)` folds `payment`, `payment_received`,
  `payment_claim_confirmed`, `refund`, `tds_credit` and `excess_payment` across **all** baselines in minor
  units (kinds WS-05 enabled carry `amount_minor`). Deposit coverage, checkout amounts, refundable amounts and
  the PayBlock use it, so superseding v1 with v2 keeps the ₹3,000 already paid and the deposit covered;
  a recorded `payment_received` covers the deposit (LT-21). Refund lookup is project-wide.
- Minor units go through `lib/minor.ts` (WS-01's ISO exponent table; no `Intl` lookups in WS-04 code).

### Confirming payments (H7, L5)
- Razorpay is enabled only when the destination has a webhook secret; otherwise `misconfigured` and the
  readiness warning in `GET /api/payment-accounts`.
- `POST /api/review/<t>/payments/razorpay/verify`: the Checkout handler's order/payment/signature, verified
  with the creating account's key secret (OAuth: partner secret), confirmed with a payment lookup and
  recorded at once; the webhook later finds it recorded.
- Stripe `success_url` carries `{CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}` and an opaque `ref` (never the review token);
  `/payment-return` asks `GET /api/payments/stripe/return` which looks the session up server-side, records
  it and returns the review link — works in a new tab.

### Recording exactly once (H8, M20, M21, H6)
- Stripe idempotency key = hash(project, purpose, target, amount_minor, open-amount version, account,
  generation); sessions expire after 30 minutes; one open session per target is reused.
- When the open amount changes, earlier Razorpay orders for that target are marked `superseded_at` (Stripe
  sessions expired). A capture beyond what is open (overpayment or on a superseded order) records the
  payment, an `excess_payment` entry (WS-05 schema; written once WS-05 has enabled the kind) and an owner
  alert with a ready refund request.
- A capture less than its order records what was paid (`expected_minor` in the entry context), marks
  `short` and alerts. Nothing is "ignored" once the order is known.
- Every verified webhook is stored in `scopeflow.payment_events` (unique provider event id) **before**
  processing; 200 only after it is stored; a processing failure answers 500 and the reconcile job
  re-processes the stored event.
- Any failure to record (WS-05 `MoneyEntryError` or otherwise) writes a `payment_alerts` row, calls
  `notify('payment_alert')` and answers 500 (webhooks, claim confirm, refund finalisation).

### Refunds (H9, H14)
- A `refunds_pending` row (unique project + request_id) is reserved inside the project lock with what is
  left computed in the lock (ledger refunds + other reservations), then the provider is called with an
  idempotency key (`X-Razorpay-Idempotency` / `Idempotency-Key`), then the entry is appended against the
  current baseline. A crash leaves a row the reconcile job resolves (asks the provider for the refund
  carrying the request key, then appends).

### Reconciliation (L4)
- Inbox first, then missing ledger entries, then pending refunds, then unpaid orders of the last 90 days
  **newest first**, at most 200 per run, with a persisted cursor (`scopeflow.payment_cursors`) so the next
  run continues; each order is looked up at most every 6 hours. Unpaid orders older than 90 days are flagged
  once for manual review (`stale_order` alert).

## New env vars
| Var | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SCOPEFLOW_ENCRYPTION_KEY` | 32-byte base64 key sealing payment-account secrets (required off localhost) |
| `SCOPEFLOW_ALLOW_OPERATOR_COLLECTION` | `1` lets operator keys take client payments (test/dev only; never in production) |
| `SCOPEFLOW_ROUTE_ENABLED` | `1` enables Razorpay Route linked accounts (only once ScopeFlow's turnover > ₹40 lakh and the payer-payee declaration is filed) |
| `RAZORPAY_PARTNER_CLIENT_ID`, `RAZORPAY_PARTNER_CLIENT_SECRET`, `RAZORPAY_PARTNER_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `RAZORPAY_PARTNER_MODE` | Razorpay Technology Partner OAuth (after partner approval) |
| `RAZORPAY_API_URL`, `RAZORPAY_OAUTH_URL` | Override Razorpay origins (test doubles) |
| `SCOPEFLOW_ESCROW_PROVIDER`, `SCOPEFLOW_ESCROW_AGREEMENT`, `CASTLER_CLIENT_ID/SECRET`, `RAZORPAYX_ESCROW_*` | Escrow adapter (off by default) |

## Operator steps
1. Set `SCOPEFLOW_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (`openssl rand -base64 32`) on the web service before any freelancer
   connects keys. Rotating it needs a re-wrap (WS-10 `lib/crypto-box.ts` once merged).
2. Keep `SCOPEFLOW_ALLOW_OPERATOR_COLLECTION` unset in production. Existing deposits/invoices that relied
   on the operator's keys now show "pay the creator directly" until the freelancer connects UPI or their own
   account; refunds of older operator-key payments still work.
3. Freelancers: Payments settings (`/payments`, or Account → Payments once WS-08 mounts it): add a UPI ID;
   optionally connect their own Razorpay keys or Stripe key and paste the shown webhook URL plus its signing
   secret into their provider dashboard (events: `payment.captured`, `order.paid`;
   `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`).
4. After Razorpay approves the Technology Partner account, set the `RAZORPAY_PARTNER_*` vars and point the
   partner webhook at `/api/payments/razorpay/webhook`.
5. Keep the daily `scripts/reconcile-payments.mjs` cron; with a backlog it continues on the next run.
